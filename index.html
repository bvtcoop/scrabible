A reward for every word of scripture

Play a biblical word and the Biblical word reward appears: the bonus, the points, and the verse it comes from.

Your score updates live as you place tiles, so you can try a word before you commit to it.

The word game for Bible experts

From Abraham to Zacchaeus, the patriarchs, prophets, places and parables all count, and the more you know, the more you score.

Not an expert yet? Every reference is a small invitation to look it up.
