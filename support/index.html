Common questions

I don't feel any haptics

In the iOS Settings app, turn on Sounds & Haptics → System Haptics and Accessibility → Touch → Vibration. If Vibration is off, iOS turns off all haptics in every app. Also check that Haptics is on in Scrabible's Settings.

I can't hear any sounds

Scrabible's sounds follow your iPhone's silent switch, so turn silent mode off and raise the volume. You can also pick a sound style (Chimes, Organ or Brass) in Scrabible's Settings.

I bought Gems but didn't receive them

Open Scrabible again while connected to the internet: purchases that didn't finish are completed when the app starts. If the Gems still don't appear, send us a message with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do online games work?

Online games use Apple's Game Center. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app, then choose Online games on the Home screen to invite a friend or play a random opponent. You have 7 days for each move.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Your level, statistics and Gems are kept in your iCloud account, so they follow you when you sign in with the same Apple Account.

A word was refused, or shouldn't be allowed

During a game, tap the ••• button and choose Report a word, or use the form above. We review every report.

What is Family mode?

Family mode, in Scrabible's Settings, blocks the few biblical words that aren't family-friendly. It applies to new games.
