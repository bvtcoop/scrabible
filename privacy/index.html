Scrabible Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

Scrabible is a word game for iPhone and iPad, made by Yuliya Nekrasova, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what your choices are.

In short:

  • We collect anonymous statistics about how the game is played, so we can improve it.
  • We never collect your name, email, contacts, location, or the words you play.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites, show ads, or sell or share your data.
  • Game Center, iCloud and purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your Apple Account or payment details.

1. Anonymous usage data

To understand how the game is played and what to improve, the app sends anonymous usage events to our analytics service, PostHog, which processes them for us on servers in the United States. The events are:

  • The app being installed, updated, opened or closed. This tells us how many people play and for how long.
  • Finishing or skipping the first-launch tour.
  • A game starting or finishing: against the computer or online, the result (win, loss, draw or resigned), and the final score in a broad range such as "100–199".
  • Each move you play: how many letters the word has, the points it scored, and whether it is a biblical word. The word itself is never sent.
  • Using a hint, exchanging tiles (how many), receiving the daily Gem gift (how many Gems), opening the Gem store, and completing or being refunded a Gem purchase (which pack only).

Each installation of the app gets a random identifier. It lets us count one person's sessions as one player, and it is not linked to your name, your Apple Account, your device's advertising identifier or your Game Center account. Deleting the app removes this identifier, and reinstalling creates a new one. Our analytics service receives your device's IP address as part of the network connection, as any internet service does. We have set it up not to work out your location from that address, and we do not use the address to identify you.

These anonymous statistics are part of how the app works, so there is no setting to switch them off. To stop sending them completely, delete the app.

2. Word reports

If you use "Report a word" to tell us that a word is wrong or missing, we receive the word, the reason you choose, any note you type (up to 300 characters), and the app and dictionary versions. Reports are sent only when you tap Send. Please don't include personal information in the note.

3. Data that stays with you or with Apple

  • Game progress (your saved games, rating, level, statistics and Gem balance) is stored on your device and in your own iCloud account, so it follows you to a new iPhone or iPad. We cannot read it. You can manage it in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud.
  • Online games are played through Apple's Game Center. The moves of a game are sent through Apple to your opponent, and the app shows your opponent's Game Center name on screen. We do not store Game Center names or identifiers, and we don't send them to analytics.
  • Purchases of Gems are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app uses Apple's transaction record only to add the Gems you bought, or to remove them if Apple refunds the purchase.

Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for, collect or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We don't use your device's advertising identifier, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't sell or "share" personal information as defined by US state privacy laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act.
  • We don't combine the app's data with data from any other source.

5. How long we keep data

We keep anonymous usage data and word reports only as long as they help us improve the game, and we delete them when they are no longer needed.

6. Children

Scrabible is a game the whole family can play, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The usage data described above is anonymous and is not linked to any person. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example in a word-report note, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to know about, access or delete personal information about you. Because the data we receive is anonymous and not linked to your identity, in most cases we cannot single out data that belongs to you. You can still contact us with any request or question, and we will help as far as we can. Deleting the app stops all data collection.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect.

9. Contact
